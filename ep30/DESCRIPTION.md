## DESCRIPTION (YouTube long)
The first big air conditioner was not built to keep anyone comfortable. In 1902 a young engineer named Willis Carrier was sent to a Brooklyn printing plant where humid air made paper swell, colours misalign and ink refuse to dry. His fix was a machine that controlled the air, not only cooled it. This is the story of air conditioning: the patent, the name, the 1925 Rivoli theatre test where cardboard fans went still, the first window units, and how it reached 87 percent of American homes.
