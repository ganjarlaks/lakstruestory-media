Nicolás Maduro was captured by United States forces in Caracas on 3 January 2026 and taken to New York, where he pleaded not guilty. He is accused, and he has not been convicted of anything. This film follows how a Caracas bus driver became president of Venezuela, and what voters, observers and courts have said about it: the disputed count of 2013, the boycotted vote of 2018, the 2024 election and the two counts that were never reconciled in public, the collapse of the economy and the exodus of millions, the 2020 indictment, the 3 January operation, and the case in New York. Case status as of 25 November 2026: not guilty pleas, no verdict, a hearing on the immunity and dismissal motions on 17 December 2026, a trial date of 14 June 2027. An indictment is an accusation; the defendants are presumed innocent unless and until proven guilty. Archive news footage, official documents and witnesses in their own voices.
