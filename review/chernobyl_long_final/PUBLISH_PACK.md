## Description (draft)
Chernobyl: The Disaster They Tried To Bury. At 01:23 on 26 April 1986 Reactor Four exploded. This film follows the plant and the town built for it, the safety test and the explosion, the response (firefighters, helicopter drops, the evacuation of Pripyat, the liquidators), and what was left: the shelter, the death-toll estimates, and the argument over who was to blame.

Chapters
0:00 Cold open
0:23 A Town Built for the Plant
1:39 The Test
3:12 The Response
6:00 What Was Left
8:13 Research references

No film of the night of 26 April exists. Every shot is real 1980s archive film (Soviet documentary, RAI TG1, a Pripyat city film, an aerial of the damaged plant, a Kostin roof film) used as short excerpts under fair use; much of it was shot after the accident or before it, and the top-left label says what each shot is. Russian and Italian speech is subtitled in English by us (machine transcript, not checked by a native speaker).

Sources: INSAG-7, The Chernobyl Accident: Updating of INSAG-1 (IAEA, 1992); World Nuclear Association, Chernobyl Accident and Its Consequences, and Appendix 2 Health Impacts; Canadian Nuclear Safety Commission, Health effects of the Chornobyl accident (UNSCEAR 2008, 2017, 2018); Associated Press, 18 April 2006 (UN report of about 4,000 deaths versus Greenpeace's 90,000 estimate).
Footage (archive.org): Chernobyl Disaster parts 1 to 6 (Soviet documentary, 1986); Chernobyl aerial shot of damaged nuclear power plant (1986); Chernobyl Pripyat before 2 May 1986 (Pripyat.com film); Disastro di Chernobyl, TG1 (RAI, about 29 April 1986); Liquidators cleaning the roof (Igor Kostin, 1986); audio: Pripyat 1986 loudspeaker recording (uploaded by Nina Mel'nik; provenance not established).
Music: Brylie Christopher Oxley, solo guitar, CC0 (archive.org; 2018-04-09 d, 09, 09b, 09c and 2018-01-28). No piano, no synthetic music.
Narration: Fraser (AI-generated voice). Set 'altered or synthetic content: yes'.
