On 25 January 1971, while Uganda's president was abroad, soldiers led by General Idi Amin sealed Entebbe airport and took control of Kampala. This film follows the record of his rule: the coup, the expulsion of Uganda's Asians in 1972, the Entebbe hijacking of 1976, the fall of Kampala in 1979, and the commissions, estimates and exile that followed. Estimates of the dead run from 100,000 to 500,000, and no court ever tried him. Archive footage, official documents and witnesses in their own voices.
