This video contains archive war footage from Libya in 2011 (armed fighters, vehicles, smoke from shelling) and discusses a capture, a killing and deaths in custody. It does not show his death, bodies or wounds. Viewer discretion is advised.

Muammar Gaddafi ruled Libya for forty-two years. On 20 October 2011 he was captured near Sirte, and he was dead the same day. The United Nations commission of inquiry found he was captured alive and wounded, and could not determine whether his death was an unlawful killing; Human Rights Watch concluded he was alive in custody and that the cause of death was unclear. This film follows the road to that day: the 1969 coup, Lockerbie and the years of sanctions, the 2011 uprising, UN Resolutions 1970 and 1973, the NATO campaign, the convoy that left Sirte, the official accounts of his death, and the investigations that followed. Where accounts disagree, the film says whose account it is. He never faced a court. Libya has been divided between rival administrations since 2014; on 30 August 2026 their representatives signed a UN-backed deal to hold elections within 24 months. Archive news footage, official documents and statements in their own voices.
