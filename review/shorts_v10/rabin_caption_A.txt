4 November 1995, Tel Aviv: more than a hundred thousand people came to a peace rally, and in the footage, Yitzhak Rabin is smiling.
He told the crowd that he had always believed most people want peace and are ready to take a risk for it. At the end, the singer Miri Aloni led everyone in the Song for Peace.
