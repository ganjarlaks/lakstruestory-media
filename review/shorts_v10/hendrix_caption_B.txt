On Wednesday 16 September 1970, two days before his death, Jimi Hendrix went to Ronnie Scott's club in Soho to watch Eric Burdon play with War.
Between sets he asked if he could join them, and played a couple of songs as the night ended: the last time he played in public. A time, a cause, an open verdict. What do you remember of Jimi Hendrix?
