Was the Apple Lisa computer named after Steve Jobs's daughter? The record holds two answers.
Lisa Brennan-Jobs writes in her memoir that her father answered no; biographer Walter Isaacson reported that Jobs said, near the end of his life, that it was obviously named for his daughter.
