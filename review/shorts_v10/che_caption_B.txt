How many men died under Che Guevara's command at La Cabaña in 1959? The sources disagree.
Biographer Jon Lee Anderson counts 55; the exile group Cuba Archive counts 105. Anderson wrote in 1997 that he had never found a credible case in which Guevara executed an innocent man. A fortress in 1959, a schoolhouse in 1967: which of the two men is on the poster?
