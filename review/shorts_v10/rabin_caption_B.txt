What did Israel's official inquiry find about the security failure around Yitzhak Rabin?
The Shamgar Commission's 1996 report found that the Shin Bet had failed in its main task, protecting the prime minister; the police and the Shin Bet had not coordinated their plans, and nobody had properly checked the parking area where Amir waited. The record is clear about that night. What does it tell you about the days before it?
