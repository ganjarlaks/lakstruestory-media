After Venezuela's election of 28 July 2024, the electoral council did not publish results from the individual polling stations.
The opposition put printed tally sheets online and said they showed González with about 67 percent and Maduro about 30. The Carter Center said the election did not meet international standards of electoral integrity and that it could not verify the official result. The government said the count was correct.
