Saturday 11 February 2012, the night before the Grammy Awards: Whitney Houston died at the Beverly Hilton in Beverly Hills. She was forty-eight.
Beverly Hills police said there were no obvious signs of criminal intent, and the next day the coroner's office said foul play was not suspected.
