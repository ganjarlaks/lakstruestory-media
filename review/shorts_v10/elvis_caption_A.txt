After Elvis Presley died in 1977, the laboratory readings did not agree.
A university laboratory working for the medical examiner found several drugs, including codeine, below toxic or lethal levels. A California laboratory reported codeine at about ten times a therapeutic dose, according to a 1991 Memphis magazine account, which also says a hospital pathologist told the family the findings were consistent with a drug overdose.
