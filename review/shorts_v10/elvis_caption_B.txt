In January 1980 the state medical board found Dr George Nichopoulos guilty of ten counts of overprescribing, one involving Elvis.
In the court and board records found for the film, no one is held to have caused the death of Elvis Presley. The record gives a certificate, a verdict and numbers nobody agrees on: when you think of the end of his life, who do you ask first, the doctor, or the people around him?
