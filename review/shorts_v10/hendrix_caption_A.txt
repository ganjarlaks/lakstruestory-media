At 12:45 on Friday 18 September 1970, a doctor at St Mary Abbot's Hospital in London pronounced Jimi Hendrix dead. He was twenty-seven.
The coroner, Gavin Thurston, found no evidence that Hendrix had meant to take his own life, but recorded an open verdict. An open verdict is not a finding of foul play.
