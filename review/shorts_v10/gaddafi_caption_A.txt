On 17 March 2011, UN Security Council Resolution 1973 on Libya was adopted by ten votes to none. Brazil, China, Germany, India and Russia abstained.
It authorised all necessary measures to protect civilians, including a no-fly zone. The resolution did not mention removing Gaddafi from power, and Russia and China later said that NATO had gone beyond its mandate.
