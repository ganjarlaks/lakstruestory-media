On 4 August 1972 Idi Amin announced what he called an economic war.
He ordered those who held British passports to leave, and days later widened the order to other Asians who were not Ugandan citizens, giving them ninety days, until 8 November. No court ever tried Idi Amin. Was justice ever done?
