Thirty years after the shooting of Tupac Shakur in Las Vegas, a jury reached a verdict. On 31 August 2026, in under three hours, it found Duane Davis guilty of first degree murder with a deadly weapon.
On 13 October a judge sentenced him to forty years to life. Prosecutors said he supplied the gun and ordered the attack; they did not argue that he fired it. Davis said he would appeal, and that appeal is pending.
